## APPROVAL-BASED ALTERNATIVE THAT IS BUILT AND TESTED

Everything below is local-only and never sends anything to Snapchat or
to any person. The user (shaik) sends any message himself, in the Snapchat
app, only after approving the exact draft text.

1. Manual contact only. Add a candidate ONLY if shaik supplies the
   Snapchat username himself AND one basis applies: she contacted him
   first, she is already a mutual / accepted contact, or shaik already
   knows her personally. No discovery, no bulk add, no cold outreach
   generated by the system.
2. Never infer. Religion, age, location, gender or intentions are NEVER
   assumed from a name, username, photo or surname. They are recorded
   ONLY when voluntarily disclosed in conversation, and marked
   "unknown" until then.
3. Adult gate. No romantic track starts unless adult status (18+) is
   voluntarily confirmed. Target range in the request is 18-25, but age
   is recorded only as volunteered, never guessed.
4. Disclosure + consent gate. The first draft is exactly:
   "Assalamualaikum ji 😊 Main Siraaj ki taraf se ek AI introduction
   assistant hoon. Agar aap comfortable ho toh kya hum thodi baat kar
   sakte hain?"
   No further AI draft is created until she explicitly agrees to
   AI-assisted conversation. If she declines, the track closes.
   The system never impersonates shaik — every draft is labelled as
   from an AI introduction assistant on his behalf, for his approval.
5. No spam. At most one draft per step. No follow-up is sent to a
   non-replier. After 48 hours with no reply, only a reminder TO SHAIK
   is generated, with at most one follow-up draft for his approval,
   then the track auto-closes.
6. Guntur filter. Only after consent, the draft question is:
   "Aap Guntur se ho kya?"
   If she confirms Guntur, continue. If she says outside Guntur, the
   romantic track ends politely — no further romantic drafts.
   Never collect an exact home address.
7. Compatibility — observed evidence only. Positive signals, incoming
   message count, and mutual effort are counted from conversation
   shaik imports/pastes himself. No personality trait is assumed.
   PROMISING requires ALL of: adult confirmed, Muslim voluntarily
   confirmed, Guntur confirmed, mutual romantic interest expressed,
   serious-relationship intention expressed, >=4 incoming messages,
   >=3 positive signals, mutual effort observed. Then the workflow
   NOTIFIES shaik and PAUSES for him to take over personally.
8. Intimacy values. Only after trust, and only with her permission to
   discuss the topic. Never demand, investigate, or try to verify
   sexual history. If she declines, the topic closes and is recorded
   only as "declined to discuss". No detail is recorded beyond what
   she voluntarily states about her own values.
9. Marriage expectations. Only after genuine mutual interest, the
   respectful question from the request may be drafted. Her stated
   view is recorded as stated. Elopement is NEVER required, and no
   one is pressured to commit, elope, or persuade family against
   their will. Disagreement ends the pressure, not the respect.
10. Privacy / delete. All data is local JSON in `data/` in this
    project only. `delete_all()` wipes candidates and conversations.
    Pause/resume is a local flag in `config.json`.

  or send messages (no permitted integration exists for those),

"Run up to set me girlfriend" is therefore understood as: keep this
approval-only project active in Muse (manual drafts, tracking,
notification + pause on a Promising match) until shaik himself says
he has found a partner and wants it closed. It is NOT a GitHub
Actions job that hunts for matches in the background. Any GitHub
repo for this personal project should preferably be PRIVATE, because
it contains his dating preferences and drafts — visibility needs
his explicit choice before any push.
